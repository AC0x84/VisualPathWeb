About Us

Visual Path was founded by two students, Azaryas Chombe and Abel Mulat, who recognized the challenges students face when looking for textbooks online. We wanted to create a platform that removes the hassle of searching multiple websites and instead provides a one-stop solution. Our mission is to help students succeed by ensuring they have the learning resources they need at their fingertips.

We are Grade 9 students from Betelhem Secondary School, both 15 years old. We built this website using HTML, and it took us over 15 days to complete. As future software engineers, we are passionate about technology and innovation. Our dream is to help our country advance in the field of technology as we grow older, making it more modern and efficient. We are committed to using our skills to contribute to a better, tech-driven future for our nation.
